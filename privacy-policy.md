\## 3. Information we collect

\*\*Account.\*\* Your name, email address, profile photo and sign-in method (Google, email and password, or Apple where available). We never see your Google or Apple password.



\*\*Profile (optional).\*\* Institution, field of study, country, research interests, short bio, and your default academic level and citation style.



\*\*Content you give us.\*\* Chat messages, research topics and settings, uploaded documents (DOCX or PDF), uploaded datasets (CSV or Excel), images you attach or take with your camera, and text you dictate by voice.



\*\*Content we create for you.\*\* AI responses, research chapters, manuscripts, abstracts, reference lists, statistical results, generated images and saved research projects.



\*\*Long-term memory.\*\* Short facts and summaries taken from your conversations and research projects, saved to your account so the assistant can use them later. You can view and delete them in the App.



\*\*Subscription information.\*\* Purchases are handled by your app store and by RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status, not your payment card details.

\## 4. How we use your information

\- To run the App's features and generate the content you request.

\- To check references against public scholarly databases.

\- To save your projects, conversations and memories to your account.

\- To manage subscriptions, keep the service secure and fix problems.



We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your content for advertising.



\## 5. Who processes your information

We use these services to run the App. Your content is sent to them only as needed for the feature you use:

\- \*\*Anthropic\*\*: AI text generation and web search for literature.

\- \*\*OpenAI\*\*: image generation from the prompts you submit.

\- \*\*Google (Firebase and Google Cloud)\*\*: sign-in, database, file storage and servers.

\- \*\*RevenueCat, Google Play and the Apple App Store\*\*: subscriptions and purchases.

\- \*\*Crossref, PubMed (NCBI) and DOAJ\*\*: reference checks, which receive source titles and journal identifiers, not your own writing.
