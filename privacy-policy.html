Scholivance Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Scholivance (formerly Public Health Matters) is a mobile app that gives health and research information, an AI assistant, research and M&E tools, and discipline guides. It is operated by GIDA KULIMI TECHNOLOGIES, Nigeria ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we handle, why, and the choices you have. Questions: emmanuelkulimi@gmail.com.

1. Important health notice

Scholivance gives general information and AI-generated answers. It is not a doctor and does not give a diagnosis or replace professional medical, legal or other advice. In an emergency, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest health facility.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, answer your questions, generate research and reports, and save your work.
  • To keep it safe and fair: prevent abuse, apply usage limits, fix errors and improve reliability.
  • To manage subscriptions and premium features.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use your conversations for advertising.

4. Services that process your information

We use trusted providers to run Scholivance. They process information only to provide their service to us:

  • Google Firebase (sign-in, database, file storage, hosting of our servers).
  • Anthropic: AI text generation and web search for literature. OpenAI: image generation from prompts you submit, and, where selected, AI text generation. The text and files you send to AI features, and relevant saved memories, are sent to these providers to produce answers. Deep research splits your question into several parts and uses these providers' web search to find sources; we keep the report and its source links in the chat, not the web pages themselves. Do not include information you do not want processed this way.
  • Crossref, PubMed (NCBI), DOAJ and OpenAlex receive source titles, identifiers and search terms when references are checked or looked up, not your own writing.
  • RevenueCat, Google Play and Apple handle subscriptions and payments.
  • Google and Apple if you choose those sign-in options.
  • Google Drive and Google Calendar, only if you connect your Google account (section 6).

Your information may be processed in countries other than Nigeria, including where these providers operate.

5. Optional chat backup

Chat backup is off by default. If you switch it on in your Profile, we save a copy of your AI Health Assistant conversations (the text of your messages and the assistant's replies, with chat titles and times) in our cloud storage, linked to your account, so you can restore them on a new phone. Pictures and files you attach are not included.

Your backup is tied to your account and cannot be read by other users. We use it only for backup and restore. Access by our team is limited to what is needed to run and secure the service.

Switching backup off in Profile deletes your cloud copy; your chats stay on your phone. If you delete a single chat while backup is on, its content is removed and a small empty marker is kept so your other devices can delete it too. Deleting your account permanently removes all your backed-up chats. Backups are limited to 300 chats per account. Backups are protected in transit and at rest by our cloud provider but are not end-to-end encrypted. Please avoid typing sensitive identifiers such as national ID numbers or passwords into chats.

6. Optional connected Google account

You can connect your Google account in Profile → Connected apps. This is off by default and you choose each service separately.

  • Google Drive: you allow Scholivance to create files and to open the files it created. It cannot see your other Drive files or folders.
  • Google Calendar: you allow Scholivance to view and edit events on your calendars.

Scholivance acts only when you tap a button, for example to save an export to Drive or to add an event. It does not read your Drive or calendar in the background. When you tap, the text of the file or the details of the event pass through our servers to Google and we do not keep a copy. The Connected apps screen also lists the files Scholivance saved for you and your events for the next 14 days; these are fetched from Google when you open the screen and are not stored by us.

To keep the connection working we store, linked to your account, an encrypted Google access token, which services you allowed and when you connected. We never see your Google password. We do not use this connection or what it returns for advertising, for training AI models, or for any purpose other than the action you asked for, and we do not share it with other providers.

Scholivance's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting. Tap Disconnect Google in Connected apps. This asks Google to cancel the access and deletes our stored record. You can also remove Scholivance at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and deleting your Scholivance account does both. Files already saved in your Drive and events already in your calendar stay in your Google account until you delete them there.

Share by email. This only opens your own email app with the text filled in. We do not send, receive or see the message.

7. Data stored on your phone

Chats, images you generate or save, your assistant settings (persona, custom instructions, read-aloud and talk-mode choices) and some work are stored on your device, separated by account. When you edit a question in a chat, the earlier version of that question and its replies is kept on your phone so you can switch back to it; these earlier versions are not included in the optional chat backup, and pictures are not kept in them. Signing out or switching accounts keeps each account's data apart. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes data that is stored only on your device.

Temporary chat. A chat started as a temporary chat is not saved in your chat history, is not backed up, and does not use or create memories. Your messages are still sent to our AI provider to produce the answer, and usage counts still apply. Sharing. When you share a chat, answer or report, it goes through your phone's share menu to the app you choose; we do not receive it and have no control over where it goes next.

8. M&E and organisation data

If you use the M&E tools to collect data for an organisation or programme, the organisation that created the project decides what is collected and who can access it, and is responsible for having consent from the people the data is about. Roles control who can view, enter, approve and export. Reports hide very small groups to protect privacy. Do not collect more personal information than your project needs.

9. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your information while your account is active.
  • You can delete individual chats, memories, projects and datasets in the app.
  • You can delete your account from within the app, or by emailing us. This permanently removes your account and the data we hold about you, including research projects, memories, summaries, analysis history, datasets, chat backups, your stored Google connection (which we also ask Google to revoke) and usage records. Some records may remain briefly in routine backups of our providers, or where we must keep them by law.
  • We act on deletion requests within 30 days.

10. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, to object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where we rely on it. We handle personal data in line with Nigeria's data protection law (the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023) and other laws that apply to you. Contact emmanuelkulimi@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or the authority where you live.

11. Security

We use access controls, authenticated requests and encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, so please keep your sign-in details private.

12. Children

You must be at least 15 to use Scholivance. If you are under 18, you confirm that a parent or guardian permits you to use the app and to give us your information. If you believe a younger child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
